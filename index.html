About

Human-centered NLP for useful intelligence.

My research explores how language technologies can become more context-aware, emotionally responsive, and aligned with human goals. I am especially interested in systems that can maintain useful memory, adapt to domain expertise, and support richer collaboration between humans and AI agents.

This GitHub Pages version is based on my Google Sites profile and is designed as a fast, editable, publication-oriented academic homepage.
